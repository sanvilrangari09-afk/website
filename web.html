About TypeRush

What this tool does and how it works.

What is TypeRush?

TypeRush is a simple typing speed test. Pick a duration, type the given passage as accurately and quickly as you can, and see your live WPM, accuracy, and error count.

How WPM is calculated

WPM (Words Per Minute) = (correctly typed characters ÷ 5) ÷ time elapsed in minutes. Dividing by 5 is the standard convention since the average English word length is about 5 characters.

How Accuracy is calculated

Accuracy = (correctly typed characters ÷ total characters typed) × 100.

Features

  • 15s / 30s / 60s test durations
  • Live WPM, accuracy, and error tracking
  • Best score saved on your device
  • Full attempt history on the Leaderboard tab
  • Copy/paste disabled to keep results honest
